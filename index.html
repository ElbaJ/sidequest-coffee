Your neighborhood coffee shop, with a shelf full of games.

Come in for a coffee. Stay for an unexpectedly good afternoon.

Side Quest Coffee Co. is a real neighborhood coffee shop, and analog tabletop games are a big part of why people stay. We have shelves of board games, cards, dice, and chess sets, plus a big community table in the middle of the room. People come in to grab a coffee and end up playing something with people they just met.

You do not need to be "a board-game person." Some of our customers play every week. Some haven't played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are welcome, and so are remote workers, students, parents, regulars, and anyone who needs a good spot for a meeting.

Recurring Events

  • Open Table Thursday: every Thursday, 6:30 to 9:30 PM. Bring your own game or grab one from our shelves. It's fine to come alone.
  • Chess & Coffee: every Sunday, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Casual. You absolutely do not have to be good at chess.
  • Learn-a-Game Night: the first Wednesday of each month, 7:00 to 8:30 PM. A staff member picks a game and teaches it. A great place to start if you're new to tabletop games.
